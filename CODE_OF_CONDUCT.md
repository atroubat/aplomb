# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's financial or personal data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption,
and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful. Security reports should use the private process in `SECURITY.md`.
Other conduct concerns may be reported privately to a maintainer through their
GitHub profile.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project or its community.
